---
title: "Category Failure Analysis"
---

We should study the companies that already tried to serve this market and failed, stalled, moved upstream, changed models, or disappeared. The goal is not to produce a graveyard of companies Rule & Form believes it is smarter than. The goal is to identify recurring failure mechanisms: customer acquisition cost, churn, support burden, custom fulfillment, low engagement, weak differentiation, dependence on setup fees, inability to standardize, or simply better economics higher in the market. For each company, we should record what it sold, to whom, at what price, how it acquired customers, how delivery worked, what appears to have gone wrong, what Rule & Form does differently, and most importantly what risk we still share with it. If several failed businesses point to the same constraint, that constraint belongs in our operating model, not in a footnote.
