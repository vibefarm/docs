- **Canonical entity data:** business name, services, service areas, credentials, hours, offers, phone, locations, languages, financing, etc. generated from the Business Record.
- **Technical search foundation:** crawlable HTML, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots directives, hreflang, metadata, semantic page structure.
- **Structured data:** LocalBusiness/Organization, Service, FAQ where appropriate, breadcrumbs, reviews where valid, locations, contact information, `sameAs`, area served, and whatever relevant standards emerge.
- **Listings consistency:** Google Business Profile, Bing, Apple, major directories, and future structured discovery surfaces should receive consistent Business Record data rather than becoming separate manually maintained truths.
- **LLM accessibility:** explicitly ensure legitimate AI/search crawlers can access the intended public content. `llms.txt` and similar conventions belong here **if they prove useful**, but we should not mistake an experimental text file for the strategy.
- **Machine-readable outputs:** JSON-LD and potentially dedicated structured endpoints/feeds generated from the same Business Record.
- **AI recommendation readiness:** clear entity identity, specific service pages, geographic specificity, authoritative business facts, useful first-party content, citations/proof, and consistent external signals.
- **Agent/action readiness:** architecture that could eventually let an agent determine services, locations, availability, pricing/estimates where appropriate, and initiate actions like booking.
- **Commerce/feed readiness:** not particularly important for a plumber today, but the architecture should be capable of supplying structured catalogs/feeds when a vertical needs them. **Shopify is exactly the extreme example worth studying here.**
- **Monitoring:** schema errors, crawlability, listing inconsistencies, AI/search referral traffic, citations/recommendations, and periodic tests against major assistants/search systems.
- **No bullshit:** we never promise “rank #1 in ChatGPT.” We promise that we will keep customer sites **technically and structurally ready for how discovery is evolving.**
