# The Real Alternatives

Some competitors are constrained by incentives. Others are constrained by capability. A lead marketplace has little reason to make a contractor less dependent on purchased leads. A traditional agency built around setup fees, retainers, and billable production cannot remove large categories of human work without changing its own economics. A generic HighLevel agency has a different problem: automation may be economically attractive, but building a maintained data model, publishing system, provisioning engine, and custom product layer requires software capability many agencies were never built to develop. These are different kinds of gaps, but both help explain why obvious customer frustrations can survive for years.
