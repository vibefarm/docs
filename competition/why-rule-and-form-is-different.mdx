# Why Rule & Form Is Different

Rule & Form is not betting that competitors are stupid or that the market somehow failed to notice contractors need better websites and follow-up. The bet is that solving the problem properly requires a different operating model. Structured intake removes interpretation. A maintained Business Record prevents the same business from being rediscovered on every change. Reusable deployment logic replaces repeated custom production. Provider infrastructure handles commodity plumbing. Human work remains, but it is deliberately pushed toward exceptions rather than being the mechanism that produces every customer account. The distinction is not a longer feature list. It is what the company must do again for customer 100 that it already did for customer 1.
