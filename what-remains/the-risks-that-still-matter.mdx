# The Risks That Still Matter

There is a possibility the gap is empty rather than protected. Small contractors may be difficult customers for reasons Rule & Form cannot engineer away. Some will close, take jobs elsewhere, become too busy to care, hand operations to a spouse, or cancel for reasons unrelated to product quality. Acquisition cost may still be too high for a \$299 relationship. Support burden may remain too unpredictable. Contractors may fail to complete even a well-designed intake without human chasing. And companies that become good at serving this market may discover that larger contractors pay far more for similar work with lower churn. These are not positioning problems. They are empirical risks. The purpose of the first cohorts is to find out whether Rule & Form's tooling changes the economics enough to overcome them.
