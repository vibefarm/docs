# What We Measure

Support minutes per active account per month should be measured as seriously as launch hours. Every customer interaction that requires human work should eventually be classified by account, category, duration, cause, whether it was standard or exceptional, and whether the resolution created something reusable. Intake deserves the same scrutiny: completion rate, time to completion, abandonment point, fields that require assistance, and human minutes spent chasing missing information. Structured intake only improves economics if customers can actually complete it without creating another form of manual fulfillment. The important curve is not simply “automation increased.” It is whether total human effort per retained customer keeps acquiring a ceiling.
