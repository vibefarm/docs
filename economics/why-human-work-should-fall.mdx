# Why Human Work Should Fall

Build labor is only half the problem. The harder risk may be the long tail after launch: change my hours, my phone is not ringing, add this photo, my nephew says the site needs something, reconnect my calendar, change this service. Software can compress production quickly. Human anxiety and support requests do not automatically compress with it. Rule & Form therefore has to reduce two separate forms of labor: the work required to launch an account and the work required to keep that account healthy every month afterward. If launch hours fall but support remains unbounded, the model still fails. Productization is successful only when the common support patterns also become defaults, rules, automation, better interfaces, or clearly bounded service.
