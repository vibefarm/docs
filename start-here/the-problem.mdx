# The Problem

The gap has persisted for reasons that are more structural than “nobody noticed.” Much of the market makes money from individual pieces of the problem rather than from eliminating it. Lead marketplaces make money when contractors continue buying leads. Traditional web shops make money from large upfront builds. Agencies make money from recurring human work. The contractor experiences these as disconnected vendors and handoffs, but for the vendors involved, the fragmentation can be perfectly rational. Rule & Form starts from a different premise: the goal is not to sell another piece of the system. It is to reduce the number of pieces the contractor has to manage at all.
