title: "Why This Gap Can Close Now"
---

The uncomfortable possibility is that this market has not remained poorly served because incumbents missed an obvious opportunity. It may have remained poorly served because small contractors were historically expensive to acquire, expensive to onboard, expensive to support, and too prone to churn for high-touch service to work at a low monthly price. Rule & Form is a bet that the underlying economics have changed. Modern publishing infrastructure lowers production cost. Structured intake reduces discovery and copywriting work. Reusable vertical systems reduce deployment variance. APIs let us rent communications, payments, calendars, and other infrastructure rather than rebuilding them. AI can remove additional production work. Channel distribution may lower acquisition cost. None of that proves the economics work. It explains why a segment that may have been unattractive to serve well before could become attractive now. The company succeeds only if the measurements prove that those curves actually moved.
