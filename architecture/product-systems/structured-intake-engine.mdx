Structured Intake turns an unfamiliar business into structured Rule & Form state.

It does not ask the customer to design a website, write marketing copy, or explain a trade Rule & Form already understands. It begins from a maintained Vertical Blueprint, asks the business where it matches or differs, captures exact facts and exceptions, and produces validated input for the Business Record.

optional context ↓ AI suggestions ↓ human confirmation ↓ Structured Intake

The engine is reusable across trades.

The blueprint is what makes it know plumbing.

The application renders a reusable intake system from versioned category configuration and shared field definitions.

The core rule

The system should already know the category. The business should only have to tell us where it differs.

For a supported vertical, intake begins from an existing model of the category.

A plumbing company does not receive a blank field asking:

What services do you offer?

It receives the relevant plumbing service model:

\[ Drain Cleaning \] \[ Water Heater Repair \] \[ Water Heater Installation \] \[ Tankless Water Heaters \] \[ Sewer Repair \] \[ Sewer Replacement \] \[ Leak Detection \] \[ Repiping \] \[ Fixtures \] \[ Commercial Plumbing \]

\[ \+ Something else \]

The customer recognizes the business instead of composing it.

This principle applies beyond services:

Services Customer types Specialties Availability Proof Credentials Offers Financing Languages Service areas Contact preferences Operational preferences Existing software

Freeform input remains available where the model cannot represent reality.

It is the escape hatch, not the default interface.

Vertical Blueprints

A supported trade resolves to a specific Vertical Blueprint version.

vertical plumbing

blueprint plumbing.v1

The blueprint defines the category-specific choices and relationships the intake engine needs.

At minimum, a selectable blueprint entry has:

canonical ID display label category aliases active state sort / grouping information optional clarification conditional relationships

For example:

ID svc.plumbing.drain\_cleaning

Label Drain Cleaning

Category service

Aliases Drain Service Clogged Drains Drain Clearing

The canonical ID is durable.

The label may improve later without changing the meaning of existing customer state.

svc.plumbing.drain\_cleaning ↓ "Drain Cleaning"

later

svc.plumbing.drain\_cleaning ↓ "Drain & Clog Cleaning"

The business remains attached to the same concept.

Two plumbing companies may initially see almost the same service catalog.

That is expected.

Plumbing Blueprint v1 ↓ Company A Company B Company C Company D

Personalization may change:

suggested selections initial ordering which conditional questions become relevant which sections receive more emphasis which optional questions can be skipped

It does not create a new plumbing ontology for every customer.

Personalization resolves the business against the model. It does not reinvent the model.

Question model

Intake questions have stable identity and explicit behavior.

A question definition should be able to describe:

question ID target field or concept input type label supporting text requiredness available choices applicability conditions validation rules ordering review behavior

Question identity does not come from the visible wording.

question ID intake.services.offered

visible label v1 Which services do you offer?

visible label v2 What kind of work do you handle?

Changing copy must not invalidate saved intake state.

Input types

Use the narrowest interaction that accurately captures the answer.

Input	Use Multi-select	Services, specialties, customer types Single-select	One bounded choice Boolean / toggle	Explicit yes or no Ordered selection	Priorities or preferred services Text	Exact short facts Number	Years, counts, quantities Address / geography	Locations and service areas Date / time	Relevant exact scheduling facts Upload	Logos, photos, credentials, assets Freeform	Genuine exceptions or business identity

Do not turn every fact into a chip merely because chips are visually convenient.

bounded domain → selection

exact fact → structured field

exception / identity → freeform Selection is factual input, not content approval

Selecting a service establishes that the service applies to the business.

It does not approve a marketing paragraph.

\[✓ Water Heater Repair\]

means

Business offers Water Heater Repair

It does not mean:

Customer approved this generated Water Heater Repair description

Finished marketing copy normally stays out of the intake flow.

A choice may contain a short clarification when the category term itself could be misunderstood:

\[ Trenchless Sewer Repair \]

Repair or replace underground sewer lines without a traditional full-length excavation.

That text explains the choice.

It is not the website copy.

The separation is deliberate:

INTAKE What is true?

↓

BUSINESS RECORD What applies to this business?

↓

CONTENT STATE How should Rule & Form express it?

↓

PUBLISHED OUTPUT What should the customer see?

The contractor is authoritative about the business.

Rule & Form is responsible for presenting the business well.

Conditional progression

Intake is conditional.

Questions appear because preceding normalized answers make them relevant.

Water Heater Services ↓ selected

Which do you handle?

\[ Tank \] \[ Tankless \]

↓ tankless selected

Do you install new tankless systems?

A company that never selected water-heater work never sees the follow-up questions.

Conditional logic reduces customer effort without reducing the detail of the final record.

Conditions use structured state

Conditions evaluate normalized answers and canonical IDs.

Prefer:

if services contains svc.plumbing.water\_heater

Avoid:

if button label == "Water Heaters"

and avoid UI-component state becoming business logic.

Hidden questions are not secretly required

A question that does not apply to the current business cannot block completion.

Requiredness is evaluated after applicability.

applicable? ↓ yes required? ↓ yes answered?

This prevents conditional branches from creating invisible validation failures.

Conditions must remain understandable

V1 does not require a generic rules language capable of expressing arbitrary programs.

Use the smallest declarative condition model required by the real intake.

Examples:

selected not selected equals not equals contains has value missing value

Add complexity only when real vertical behavior requires it.

Priority is different from availability

What a company offers and what it wants to emphasize are separate facts.

A plumber may offer twenty services but want most new work in three.

OFFERS

Drain Cleaning Water Heater Repair Repiping Sewer Repair Leak Detection Fixtures Commercial Plumbing ...

PRIORITY

1. Water Heater Replacement
2. Sewer Repair
3. Repiping

Do not infer priority merely from selection order unless the UI explicitly tells the user that order has meaning.

The Business Record must be able to distinguish:

we do this

from

we want more of this

That difference can later influence homepage hierarchy, service prominence, lead treatment, and campaign strategy without misrepresenting what the business offers.

AI-assisted resolution

AI may help resolve messy business language against the known blueprint.

It does not become the authority for business facts.

A business may optionally write:

"We're a family-owned residential plumbing company. Most of our work is drains, water heaters and sewer. We answer calls in the evenings but don't really operate as a 24/7 emergency company."

The system may suggest:

Suggested

\[✓ Residential\] \[✓ Drain Cleaning\] \[✓ Water Heater Services\] \[✓ Sewer Work\] \[✓ Family Owned\] \[✓ Evening Availability\]

Not suggested

\[  Commercial\] \[  24/7 Emergency\]

These are suggestions.

They become business facts only after confirmation.

freeform context ↓ AI classification ↓ suggested canonical IDs ↓ customer / staff confirms ↓ intake answer ↓ Business Record AI may not create unverified claims

AI cannot silently infer facts such as:

Licensed Insured 24/7 Emergency Financing Available 40 Years in Business Same-Day Service Background-Checked Technicians Warranty

from tone, category norms, website language, or probability.

If a claim requires confirmation, it remains unconfirmed until an authoritative actor confirms it.

AI operates against the blueprint

Prefer:

Resolve this description against these known plumbing concepts.

over:

Invent the services this company probably provides.

This keeps AI useful where ambiguity exists without turning generation into category authority.

AI handles uncertainty. The product handles what is already known.

Imported information

Existing public information may accelerate intake.

Potential sources include:

existing website Google Business Profile structured business data uploaded documents staff-entered information

Imported information is not automatically trusted merely because it was found publicly.

The engine distinguishes:

observed suggested confirmed

For example:

Existing site mentions: "24-hour plumbing"

↓

Imported suggestion: 24/7 Emergency

↓

Customer confirmation required

↓

confirmed fact

This prevents stale websites, directory errors, old offers, and AI extraction mistakes from silently becoming canonical business truth.

Custom answers and outliers

The blueprint will not represent every real business.

A known selection uses the canonical ID.

service\_id svc.plumbing.drain\_cleaning

custom\_label null

An unrecognized service preserves exactly what the business supplied:

service\_id null

custom\_label Backflow Testing & Certification

Do not force an unusual answer into the nearest known category simply to keep the data clean.

The unresolved answer remains visible to Rule & Form.

custom answer ↓ preserved as entered ↓ review ↓

one-off → remains custom

recurring pattern → candidate blueprint addition

This is how exceptions become product knowledge without corrupting the taxonomy.

Unsupported verticals

V1 does not need to autonomously create a new Vertical Blueprint because someone selects Not listed.

An unsupported vertical can preserve the requested category and route it to a controlled fallback or review path.

Trade

\[ Plumbing \] \[ HVAC \] \[ Roofing \] \[ Electrical \] \[ Remodeling \] \[ Not listed \]

Not listed means:

Rule & Form does not yet have a trusted category model for this business.

It must not mean:

Ask AI to invent one and pretend it is equivalent.

How unsupported verticals become new supported Blueprints is a separate product process.

Intake Session

An Intake Session represents work in progress.

It is not itself the Business Record.

Organization ↓ Business ↓ Intake Session ↓ answers suggestions uploads progress review state

The session preserves enough information to:

save and resume render the correct next question retain confirmed selections retain custom answers distinguish suggestions from answers restore uploaded assets reconstruct review state audit how a canonical value entered the system where required Session identity

An Intake Session belongs explicitly to:

Organization Business Vertical Blueprint version

Tenant context is never inferred from the session ID alone.

Accounts, Tenancy & Identity owns the tenant boundary.

Save and resume

Every meaningful user action should survive navigation or browser interruption without requiring the customer to restart the flow.

answer question ↓ persist ↓ update applicable progression ↓ continue

Save and resume must not require one giant form submission at the end.

The user may safely leave after any persisted step and return to the same business context.

Blueprint version remains known

A session records the blueprint version it began against.

If the current Plumbing Blueprint changes while someone has an unfinished intake, the system does not silently reinterpret existing answers against a different model.

Intake Session Plumbing Blueprint v1.2

current system blueprint Plumbing Blueprint v1.3

Migration to a newer blueprint is an explicit operation when required.

Review and commit

Work-in-progress intake state and canonical business truth are separate concerns.

The session may contain:

unfinished answers AI suggestions unconfirmed imports custom values invalid values uploads awaiting review

Those must not silently become authoritative Business Record state.

Before canonical commit, the engine resolves:

applicability required fields field validation cross-field validation unconfirmed suggestions blocking custom cases

The resulting operation is a deliberate Business Record change.

Intake Session ↓ validated confirmed answers ↓ Business Record write path ↓ new canonical state

The Business Record page owns its versioning mechanics and mutation rules.

The intake engine does not create a competing source of truth.

Existing businesses

Structured Intake is not only an onboarding mechanism.

The same controlled input patterns may later be used to edit existing business facts.

Current Business Record ↓ hydrate relevant intake state ↓ customer / staff changes facts ↓ validate ↓ commit ↓ new Business Record version

Changing the business does not require returning to an unstructured support conversation.

That does not mean every future change must expose the full onboarding flow. Narrow edit surfaces may reuse the same field definitions and validation rules.

Validation

Validation exists at multiple levels.

Field validation

Examples:

phone format URL format required value numeric range valid email supported upload type Category validation

Examples:

Tankless installation selected but Water Heater Services absent

24/7 Emergency selected but no emergency contact path

Spanish selected but localization configuration incomplete

The engine may flag contradictions before commit.

Business-rule validation

Some rules depend on product policy rather than form mechanics.

Those rules belong to Validation & Business Rules.

The intake engine invokes them.

It does not create a second copy of the same rule inside the UI.

UI constraint → basic interaction validity

domain validation → business truth validity Uploads and media

Intake may collect:

logo team photography project photography vehicle photography licenses certifications brand assets existing collateral

The intake answer stores references to managed Rule & Form assets.

It does not make a temporary browser upload URL part of canonical business state.

upload ↓ managed asset ↓ asset identity ↓ confirmed Business Record reference

File validation, storage policy, transformation, and asset lifecycle belong to the relevant storage and content infrastructure.

Intake does not generate the website

Structured Intake terminates at structured business state.

It may trigger downstream work once enough state exists, but it does not own website generation.

Structured Intake ↓ Business Record

Business Record ↓ Content State ↓ Website Engine ↓ Framer Publishing

This boundary is important.

Do not create:

on service chip click immediately write Framer CMS

or:

intake component directly generates homepage paragraph

Selections become durable business facts first.

Downstream systems decide how those facts affect their own outputs.

One service choice may later influence many systems because those systems read the same canonical state.

Drain Cleaning selected ↓ Business Record ↓ Website Engine Search structure Lead categorization Business Hub future automation

The intake engine does not coordinate each consequence itself.

Instrumentation

Structured Intake is part of the economic model, so its performance must be measurable.

At minimum, the system should eventually support measurement of:

time to complete completion rate abandonment point time per step custom-answer rate AI suggestion acceptance rate import confirmation rate validation failure rate human-review minutes number of post-intake corrections

The purpose is not product analytics for its own sake.

These measurements answer whether Rule & Form is actually reducing repeated human work.

More customers ↓ better Vertical Blueprint ↓ fewer ambiguous answers ↓ less manual interpretation ↓ faster intake ↓ lower fulfillment cost

Instrumentation & Work Logging owns the common event and work-measurement model.

Acceptance conditions

The Structured Intake Engine is working when:

A user can choose Plumbing and receive the Plumbing Blueprint without plumbing-specific page code. Known services resolve to stable canonical IDs. Changing a display label does not change the identity of previously stored selections. Two plumbing companies can use the same blueprint while producing different business state. An optional company description can suggest known blueprint selections without confirming them automatically. No AI suggestion becomes a business fact without confirmation. Selecting Water Heater Services can reveal relevant follow-up questions. A hidden conditional question cannot block completion. A customer can add a legitimate service not represented by the current blueprint. Custom answers remain distinguishable from canonical entries. A saved session can be resumed without losing selections, custom input, or progress. An unfinished Intake Session is not treated as canonical Business Record truth. Confirmed intake can create or update Business Record state through one authoritative write path. A service selection does not directly mutate Framer, HighLevel, or another provider. Marketing copy does not need to be approved during factual service selection. Exact business facts remain structured fields rather than being buried inside prose. Unsupported verticals remain explicit instead of silently receiving an AI-invented category model. The demo can be reset to a deterministic Plumbing intake fixture.
