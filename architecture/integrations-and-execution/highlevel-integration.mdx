---

HighLevel is infrastructure behind Rule & Form.

It gives V1 mature communications, telephony, booking, and automation without requiring Rule & Form to build those systems before proving the business.

It does not define the Rule & Form domain.

<Callout icon="terminal" color="#8B5CF6">
  **For implementation** — contractors use Rule & Form, not HighLevel. Rule & Form owns domain identity, customer history, product state, and intended behavior. HighLevel resources are mapped execution infrastructure behind provider adapters. [Provider Adapters](/architecture/integrations-and-execution/provider-adapters) owns the shared boundary; this page owns HighLevel-specific behavior.
</Callout>

## Role and ownership

```text
CONTRACTOR

Business Hub
Website
Setup

      ↓

RULE & FORM

Customers
Leads
Conversations
Messages
Appointments
Capabilities
Commands
Provider Mappings

      ↓

HighLevel Adapters

      ↓

HIGHLEVEL

Contacts
Opportunities
Conversations / Transport
LC Phone
Calendars / Appointments
Selected Workflows
```

The goal is not to build headless HighLevel. The goal is to use the parts of HighLevel required by the Rule & Form operating loop.

**Availability inside HighLevel does not make a capability part of Rule & Form.**

### Ownership matrix

| Rule & Form concept | HighLevel resource | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business | Sub-account / Location | Rule & Form |
| Business Record | Custom Values where explicitly projected | Rule & Form |
| Customer | Contact | Rule & Form |
| Lead | Opportunity where required | Rule & Form |
| Conversation | Conversation | Rule & Form |
| Message content and history | Message | Rule & Form durable history |
| Delivery / transport result | Message state | HighLevel while active provider |
| Appointment | Calendar Event / Appointment | Rule & Form identity; HighLevel scheduling execution |
| Phone configuration | LC Phone resources | Rule & Form intent; HighLevel execution |
| Follow-up behavior | Workflow where used | Rule & Form intent; HighLevel execution |
| Capability State | None | Rule & Form |

The central rule is: **we are not synchronizing two CRMs.**

Rule & Form owns the model. HighLevel receives the provider representations needed to execute specific capabilities.

### Business to sub-account

V1 uses one primary HighLevel sub-account for each Business connected to HighLevel. <Badge color="blue" size="sm" stroke>V1 scope</Badge>

```text
Organization
    ↓
Business
    ↓
Integration Connection
    ↓
HighLevel Sub-Account
locationId
```

<Callout icon="lock" color="#E2E8F0">
  `locationId` is provider identity. It never becomes `business_id` or `organization_id`.
</Callout>

Do not combine unrelated Businesses inside one HighLevel sub-account merely because they share an Organization.

### Customer access

The normal contractor experience contains no HighLevel login.

```text
Contractor
    ↓
Rule & Form Business Hub

not

Contractor
    ↓
white-labeled HighLevel
```

Rule & Form staff may use HighLevel directly as an operational fallback during the early customer phase. Manual provider changes must remain visible to reconciliation.

HighLevel is an internal tool when necessary. It is not the customer product.

## Connection

Production HighLevel access belongs to an Organization-owned Integration Connection.

```text
Integration Connection

provider = highlevel
organization_id
business_id
external_resource_id = locationId

credential state
installed scopes
connection status
last verified
repair state
```

Credentials remain server-side. The browser never receives HighLevel access or refresh tokens.

The Integration Connection owns credential lifecycle. The adapter receives usable credentials for one execution attempt.

<Accordion title="Token lifecycle ownership">
  Integration infrastructure owns authorization, durable token storage, token refresh, token rotation, expiry state, revoked authorization, and reconnection requirements.

  The adapter reports authentication failure. It does not run an independent credential-repair loop.

  Authentication failure becomes explicit connection state:

  ```text
  HighLevel connection
        ↓
  authentication failed
        ↓
  repair required
        ↓
  affected capabilities recompute
  ```
</Accordion>

### Scopes

Grant only the HighLevel scopes required by the product. V1 primarily needs access around:

```text
Contacts
Conversations / Messages
Opportunities
Calendars / Appointments
required Phone resources
subscribed webhook events
```

**Do not request broad provider access merely because HighLevel exposes it.**

### Business Record projection

The Business Record does not synchronize wholesale into HighLevel.

HighLevel receives only the canonical business facts required by provider configuration, workflows, calendars, or message templates.

```text
Business Record

business name
website
selected contact facts
selected scheduling facts
selected operational values

        ↓

explicit provider mapping

        ↓

HighLevel Custom Values /
provider configuration
```

HighLevel supports Custom Values through its API, and those values can be consumed by provider features such as workflows and calendars.

**Do not create a mirrored Business Record inside HighLevel.** Project only the values provider execution actually requires.

If HighLevel requires a business value in a workflow, that value is a projection of the [Business Record](/architecture/product-systems/business-record), not a second editable truth. A manual HighLevel edit to a Rule & Form-owned projected value is drift.

## Leads and customers

### Website lead path

Website inquiries enter Rule & Form first.

```text
Website form
      ↓
Rule & Form

Customer
Lead
source / attribution

      ↓
Provider Command

      ↓
HighLevel Adapter

      ↓
Contact
Opportunity where required
```

**A valid website inquiry must survive a HighLevel outage.** The Customer and Lead are recorded before provider synchronization is attempted.

### Contacts

A Rule & Form Customer may map to a HighLevel Contact.

```text
Customer
    ↓
Provider Mapping
    ↓
HighLevel Contact
```

Once a mapping exists, do not rediscover identity from phone number or email on every operation.

HighLevel contact deduplication behavior does not define Rule & Form Customer identity. Rule & Form decides who the Customer is; the adapter decides how that Customer is represented in HighLevel.

### Opportunities

A Rule & Form Lead may map to a HighLevel Opportunity where provider workflows or internal fallback require one.

```text
Rule & Form Lead
      ↓
Provider Mapping
      ↓
HighLevel Opportunity
```

Do not build Rule & Form product logic around HighLevel pipeline IDs. A Lead remains valid without an Opportunity if that provider representation is not required.

### Business Hub read path

The Business Hub reads Rule & Form state. It is not a live HighLevel API viewer.

| Business Hub | Rule & Form state | HighLevel role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Capabilities, activity, leads | Supplies some execution events |
| Leads | Customer \+ Lead | Contact / Opportunity representation |
| Inbox | Conversation \+ Messages | Transport |
| Schedule | Appointment | Availability / booking execution |
| Website | Site \+ Content State | None |
| Setup | Capability State | Phone / calendar / connection reality |

## Communication

### Conversation and message history

Rule & Form stores the durable communication history required by the Business Hub.

```text
Message

organization
business
conversation

channel
direction

sender / recipient identity
body

occurred_at
received_at

provider mapping
provider transport state
```

<Callout icon="lock" color="#E2E8F0">
  Message content, direction, participants, and timestamps remain available if the active communications provider changes. HighLevel owns transport execution while it is the provider. Rule & Form owns the durable customer history.
</Callout>

```text
Rule & Form Conversation
      ↓
Rule & Form Messages
      ↓
Provider Mapping
      ↓
HighLevel transport
```

Provider delivery IDs and transport status remain mapped provider state.

Attachments that must remain part of durable history are copied into Rule & Form-controlled asset storage. **A temporary provider URL is not durable history.**

### Inbound communication

```text
Incoming SMS / Call
        ↓
HighLevel
        ↓
webhook
        ↓
Provider Event
        ↓
Rule & Form Conversation / Message
        ↓
Business Hub Inbox
```

The Business Hub reads local Rule & Form state rather than requesting the entire conversation from HighLevel every time the screen loads.

### Sending a reply

```text
Contractor writes reply
        ↓
Rule & Form Message / Command
        ↓
HighLevel Messaging Adapter
        ↓
provider transport
        ↓
delivery result
        ↓
Rule & Form transport state
```

The browser never sends directly through HighLevel.

### Outbound phone identity

A reply leaves from the same customer-facing Rule & Form-managed number associated with that conversation.

```text
Customer calls

(325) 555-1000

        ↓

conversation associated
with that number

        ↓

SMS reply

from
(325) 555-1000
```

**Do not arbitrarily switch customers between provider numbers.**

Where several sending numbers exist, the conversation retains enough routing context to choose the intended outbound identity. HighLevel's send-message API supports an explicit `fromNumber`, so Rule & Form selects that identity rather than relying on a provider default.

### V1 phone model

The safest initial phone configuration is a dedicated local website tracking number.

```text
Website visitor
      ↓
website phone number
      ↓
HighLevel / LC Phone
      ↓
forwarding
      ↓
contractor's existing phone
```

The contractor keeps the phone system they already use. **Rule & Form does not require porting an established business number before launch.**

Calls placed directly to the contractor's old number remain outside Rule & Form unless that number is later integrated deliberately.

### Missed-call response

Missed-call response is managed behavior, not a provider toggle.

```text
Missed call
    ↓
eligible for automatic response?
    ↓
YES
    send response

repeat call inside suppression window
    ↓
do not send duplicate response
```

Rule & Form owns the suppression and follow-up rule. HighLevel may execute it.

### A2P is long-lead provisioning

A phone number existing does not mean SMS is ready.

U.S. local business messaging requires A2P registration covering business identity, campaign and use-case information, and consent evidence. HighLevel warns that registration can take weeks in some cases and that carrier propagation can continue after approval.

**A2P therefore starts during onboarding, as soon as the required business information and compliant opt-in evidence exist.** Do not wait until Site publication.

```text
Structured Intake
      ↓
business identity available
consent / messaging details available
      ↓
start A2P registration

IN PARALLEL

Website build
Content
Publishing
Other provisioning
```

| Contractor provides | Rule & Form handles |
| --- | --- |
| Legal business identity | Submission |
| Required registration details | Configuration |
| Authorized representative information | Monitoring |
| Messaging use-case details where needed | Correction and resubmission |
|  | Status communication |

Consent evidence is a dependency between two Rule & Form systems, not a contractor task. Generated website forms must carry the opt-in language that A2P registration requires, and that language is part of the form specification rather than per-customer copy. The [Website System](/architecture/product-systems/website-engine) owns the form; this page owns the requirement that it exist.

Until messaging is genuinely ready:

```text
Website
    LIVE

Phone
    READY / LIVE where voice works

SMS
    IN PROGRESS
or
    ACTION NEEDED
```

**Site launch is not held hostage by carrier approval.** A valid fallback remains available.

<Accordion title="Call recording is not enabled by default">
  Provider support for call recording does not mean Rule & Form enables it.

  Recording, consent, retention, access, and disclosure require an explicit policy before production use. That policy belongs to a later security and operating pass.
</Accordion>

## Booking and automation

### Booking

HighLevel is the initial scheduling provider.

```text
Website
    ↓
Rule & Form booking endpoint
    ↓
Calendar Adapter
    ↓
provider availability
    ↓
visitor chooses time
    ↓
Rule & Form Appointment
    ↓
Provider Command
    ↓
HighLevel Appointment
```

Rule & Form owns Appointment identity. HighLevel executes availability and booking while it is the active Calendar Provider. Provider-originated changes update the mapped Appointment through verified events.

Booking does not block Site launch.

```text
Website
    LIVE

Booking
    ACTION NEEDED
```

If booking is unavailable, the Site uses a valid fallback such as calling or submitting a request. Calendar authorization, appointment types, working hours, buffers, and other requirements become explicit setup tasks.

A specialist provider such as Nylas is a possible future replacement direction, not a committed migration.

### Automation ownership

Rule & Form owns intended behavior. The contractor sees product concepts, not a workflow builder.

```text
New lead response        ON
Missed-call response     ON
Estimate follow-up       ON
Appointment reminders    ON
Past-customer follow-up  OFF
```

<Accordion title="Why HighLevel workflows are managed configuration, not a product surface">
  HighLevel's public Workflows API exposes retrieval rather than a general workflow CRUD surface. Arbitrary native workflows cannot be created and rewritten programmatically.

  V1 therefore treats selected workflows as managed provider configuration maintained by Rule & Form, not as customer-editable product behavior. The semantic rule stays in Rule & Form — _missed-call response is enabled_ — rather than _HighLevel Workflow X is the product_.
</Accordion>

### HighLevel Snapshots

A maintained source sub-account may contain reusable HighLevel configuration.

```text
Rule & Form Plumbing Core

Pipeline
Custom Fields
Custom Values
Calendars
Message templates
Selected workflows
Tags / provider configuration
```

HighLevel Snapshots copy reusable provider configuration including workflows, calendars, custom fields, custom values, and pipelines.

Always qualify the term.

```text
HighLevel Snapshot
    reusable provider configuration

Published Snapshot
    immutable Rule & Form publishing provenance
```

They are unrelated concepts. [Naming, IDs & Conventions](/architecture/foundation/naming-ids-and-conventions) is the enforcement point.

### Manual provisioning is allowed

```text
create sub-account manually
        ↓
apply HighLevel Snapshot
        ↓
authorize connection
        ↓
record Integration Connection
        ↓
project required Business Record values
        ↓
verify
```

**Manual early provisioning is not an architectural exception. Untracked manual state is.**

Every manual provider step that affects readiness must eventually resolve into explicit Rule & Form state.

## Execution and drift

### Commands

All Rule & Form initiated HighLevel mutations use the durable command path.

```text
Rule & Form action
      ↓
domain transaction
      ↓
Provider Command
      ↓
workflow execution
      ↓
HighLevel Adapter
      ↓
HighLevel
```

Provider failure does not erase the original Rule & Form action.

### Webhooks

HighLevel webhooks are the fast path for provider-originated change.

```text
HighLevel
    ↓
signed webhook
    ↓
verify
    ↓
resolve locationId
    ↓
Integration Connection
    ↓
Organization + Business
    ↓
record Provider Event
    ↓
acknowledge
    ↓
async processing
```

New Rule & Form webhook code verifies the current `X-GHL-Signature` Ed25519 signature against the raw request body before parsing JSON. Do not build new integration logic around the legacy RSA `X-WH-Signature` path, which HighLevel identifies as transition support.

<Callout icon="lock" color="#E2E8F0">
  A valid signature proves provider origin. It does not prove tenant identity. `locationId` must still resolve through an Integration Connection before tenant-owned processing.
</Callout>

Webhook handling is intentionally short.

```text
verify
resolve connection
deduplicate
persist raw event
acknowledge

        ↓

background processing
```

Duplicate delivery must be harmless. HighLevel recommends fast acknowledgement and idempotent processing.

### Local projections

HighLevel is not the Business Hub database.

```text
HighLevel
   ↓ webhooks / reads

Provider Events
Provider Mappings
transport state

        ↓

Rule & Form
domain + projections

        ↓

Business Hub
```

This gives Rule & Form tenant-scoped reads, joins with canonical data, durable conversation history, auditability, resilience to provider latency, reconciliation, and provider replacement.

### One writer per field

Synchronization is not last-write-wins.

```text
Lead stage
    Rule & Form authoritative

HighLevel Opportunity stage
    provider projection


Message body / history
    Rule & Form durable state

Message delivery result
    HighLevel while active provider


Business phone intent
    Rule & Form

provider routing reality
    HighLevel
```

A disagreement with a Rule & Form-owned value is drift. **It is not permission for HighLevel to silently become authoritative.**

### Reconciliation

Webhooks provide freshness. Reconciliation provides correctness.

```text
Rule & Form expected state
          ↕
    HighLevel Adapter
          ↕
HighLevel actual state
          ↓
      reconcile
```

V1 reconciliation is narrow. It verifies the provider resources required by the operating loop:

- connection still works
- required mappings still exist
- recent message state can be reconciled
- Appointment mappings agree
- phone resources remain available
- required provider configuration remains present

Perfect synchronization of an entire HighLevel sub-account is deferred.

### Rate limits and failure behavior

The adapter detects provider rate limits and returns retry information. Durable workflow infrastructure owns waiting and rescheduling.

**Do not sleep inside the adapter. Do not hard-code current provider quotas into domain logic.**

A HighLevel failure degrades the affected capability. It does not corrupt unrelated Rule & Form state.

```text
HighLevel unavailable

Website
    remains live

Lead form
    stores Lead

SMS
    pending / retrying

Booking
    fallback

Business Hub
    known local state

Capability
    reflects problem
```

## Provider replacement

HighLevel currently sits behind several replaceable boundaries.

```text
Messaging

HighLevel
    ↓
Twilio / Telnyx direction


Calendar

HighLevel
    ↓
specialist provider direction


Automation

HighLevel workflows
    ↓
Rule & Form / Inngest where ownership moves
```

These are architectural directions, not migration promises. Replacement may require substantial engineering and provider migration.

It must not require redefining Business, Customer, Lead, Conversation, Message, Appointment, or Capability.

Because Rule & Form stores durable conversation history locally, replacing the transport provider does not imply throwing away historical customer conversations.

## Acceptance conditions

The integration is working when:

- A website submission creates a Rule & Form Lead before HighLevel synchronization.
- That Lead can map to provider Contact and Opportunity records without adopting their IDs.
- Message bodies remain available from Rule & Form storage.
- An inbound SMS appears in Business Hub through webhook processing.
- A Business Hub reply sends from the intended customer-facing number.
- A missed website call triggers one controlled response without duplicate spam.
- A2P can remain In progress without blocking Site launch.
- Real calendar availability produces a mapped Rule & Form Appointment.
- A failed provider command retries without duplicating the side effect.
- Duplicate webhooks create no duplicate Rule & Form activity.
- An invalid signature touches no tenant data.
- A `locationId` resolves to exactly one Integration Connection before processing.
- Expired authorization becomes connection-repair state.
- HighLevel can be unavailable without losing a website inquiry.
- A Business Record value used by HighLevel remains Rule & Form-authoritative.
- Demo and preview execution cannot reach a production customer sub-account.
- Reconciliation detects provider drift.

## Foundation rules

1. **HighLevel is a provider, not the Rule & Form product.**
2. **Contractors use Rule & Form and do not require HighLevel UI access.**
3. **One V1 Business maps to one primary HighLevel sub-account when HighLevel is enabled.**
4. **`locationId` is provider identity, never tenant or Business identity.**
5. **Rule & Form owns Customers, Leads, Conversations, Messages, Appointments, capability state, and automation intent.**
6. **Message content and customer conversation history are durable Rule & Form state; HighLevel owns transport execution while active.**
7. **Website Leads are recorded before HighLevel synchronization.**
8. **The Business Record is never mirrored wholesale; only required provider values are projected explicitly.**
9. **Credentials belong to the Integration Connection and remain server-side.**
10. **A2P is long-lead provisioning and begins as soon as required customer information exists.**
11. **Generated website forms carry the opt-in language that messaging registration requires.**
12. **Inbound webhooks are verified, tenant-resolved, deduplicated, and recorded before business processing.**
13. **Outbound mutations travel through durable Rule & Form commands.**
14. **HighLevel workflows implement selected behavior; they do not define Rule & Form automation semantics.**
15. **A HighLevel Snapshot is not a Rule & Form Published Snapshot.**
16. **Webhooks provide freshness; reconciliation repairs drift.**
17. **Provider failure degrades the affected capability without destroying canonical state.**
18. **Replacing HighLevel may require migration work but does not redefine the Rule & Form domain or erase durable conversation history.**

HighLevel earns its place by letting Rule & Form ship the operating loop now without making HighLevel the architecture.

**Rent the plumbing. Own the system.**
