title: "Final AI reviews"
---

- **Accelerator partner:** “Would you interview or reject this company? Why?”
- **Skeptical seed investor:** “Find the strongest reason this should not be funded.”
- **Vertical SaaS operator:** “Does the architecture and business model actually hang together?”
- **SMB economics reviewer:** “Where does the \$299 model break?”
- **Technical diligence:** “Is this genuinely a software layer or assembled SaaS?”
- **Contradiction audit:** “Find conflicting claims, stale assumptions, unsupported assertions, or pages that weaken the thesis.”
- **AI-only diligence:** Give it just the public Mint and links, no coaching, and see what category it assigns us.
